Before you start

What you need

Just three things. You don't need an API key, a broker login or any other software.

TradeX Pro

MT5 connections are part of the Pro plan.

MT5 on a computer

Windows, Mac or a VPS. The MT5 phone app can't run Expert Advisors.

5 minutes

You set it up once. After that it runs in the background.

1In TradeX

Create your MT5 connection and copy your token

Your token links your MT5 terminal to your TradeX account.

  1. Open PnL Calendar and click Add Exchange.
  2. Choose MT5, give it a name (e.g. "My FTMO account") and click Create MT5 connection.
  3. Click the copy icon next to Token and paste it somewhere safe, like Notepad.
Your token is shown only once. Keep this window open until you finish. Lost it? Click the key icon on your MT5 chip in TradeX to get a new one.

3In MetaEditor

Compile the EA

This turns the file into something MT5 can run. It takes about a second.

  1. In MT5, press F4 to open MetaEditor.
  2. On the left, open Experts and double-click TradexJournal. Don't see it? Right-click Experts and choose Refresh.
  3. Press F7, or click Compile.
At the bottom you should see 0 errors, 0 warnings. That's all MetaEditor is needed for, so you can close it.

4In MT5

Allow MT5 to talk to TradeX

MT5 blocks internet access for EAs until you approve a website. This is the step people miss most often.

  1. Press Ctrl + O (or Tools › Options) and open the Expert Advisors tab.
  2. Tick Allow algorithmic trading.
  3. Tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL.
  4. Double-click "add new URL", paste the address below and press Enter. Then click OK.
https://tradexterminal.online
Press Enter after pasting the URL. If you click OK while the line is still being edited, MT5 may not save it.

5In MT5

Attach the EA to one chart and paste your token

  1. Press Ctrl + N to open the Navigator, then expand Expert Advisors.
  2. Drag TradexJournal onto any one chart. The symbol doesn't matter.
  3. In the Inputs tab, double-click the empty value next to TradeX token, paste your token and press Enter.
  4. In the Common tab, tick Allow Algo Trading. Click OK.
  5. Make sure the Algo Trading button in the MT5 toolbar is green.
One chart covers the whole account. Manual trades, phone trades and trades from other EAs are all journaled too. Don't attach it twice.
Your token starts with tdx_mt5_. It's long, so the box only shows the end of it, which is normal. Leave the other inputs as they are.

6Check it's working

Look for "connected", then close a test trade

  1. The top-left of the chart should read TradeX Journal: connected.
  2. In TradeX, the setup window turns green: EA connected.
  3. On a demo account, open a 0.01-lot trade and close it.
  4. Within about 10 seconds you'll see "1 new trade journaled from MT5", and the trade appears on today's date.
Click any day on the calendar to see its trades. Trades from MT5 carry an MT5 EA badge, so you can always tell them apart from ones you logged by hand.

Troubleshooting

Seeing a message? Here's the fix.

The EA writes its status in the top-left corner of the chart. Find your message below.

FixThe token box is empty or has the wrong text in it. Copy the token again with the copy icon in TradeX (not by selecting the dots), then paste it into Inputs › TradeX token. It must start with tdx_mt5_.
On the charterror: add https://tradexterminal.online/… to Tools > Options > Expert Advisors > Allow WebRequest
FixMT5 is blocking the connection. Redo step 4 and remember to press Enter after pasting the URL. The EA retries every 15 seconds, so it connects by itself once that's fixed.
On the charterror: token rejected. Generate a new one in TradeX…
FixThe token is wrong or was replaced. In TradeX, click the key icon on your MT5 chip to get a new token, then paste it into the EA's Inputs.
Nothing on the chartNo status text, or a grey / sad face icon at the top right

FAQ

Good questions

Is it safe? Can it place trades on my account?

No. The EA contains no trading code at all. It only reads your deal history and sends closed trades (symbol, direction, profit, swap, commission and close time) to your TradeX account. It never sees your MT5 password.

Does MT5 need to stay open?

Yes, trades are sent while MT5 is running. If you close it, nothing is lost: on the next start the EA resends your closed trades from the last 30 days, and TradeX skips any it already has. For always-on journaling, run MT5 on a VPS.

Will trades from my phone or other EAs be journaled?

Yes. The EA watches the whole account, so trades placed from the MT5 mobile app, other EAs or copy-trading are journaled too, as long as MT5 with the EA is running on your computer.

I have more than one MT5 account. What do I do?

Create one MT5 connection per account in TradeX. Each gets its own token. Attach the EA once in each terminal (or each account's chart) with that account's token, and use the calendar's filter to view accounts separately or together.

Does it work with prop firms (FTMO, The5ers, etc.)?

Yes, for any account that runs in the MT5 desktop terminal. The EA doesn't trade, so it doesn't conflict with prop firm rules about EAs. Check your firm's terms if you're unsure.

What if I switch computers or reinstall MT5?

Repeat steps 2 to 5 on the new computer. You can reuse the same token if you still have it. If not, get a new one with the key icon. Your journaled trades stay in TradeX either way.

How do I stop it?

Remove the EA from the chart (right-click the chart › Expert list › Remove). To disconnect completely, delete the MT5 chip in TradeX. Note that deleting the connection also removes the trades it journaled.
